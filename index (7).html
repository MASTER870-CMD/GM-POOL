A consolidated team, focused on customers.

GM Pools is one of the leading swimming pool construction and refurbishment companies located in Bangalore. We also supply swimming pool equipment and accessories, chemicals and all types of pool tiles, and we undertake maintenance contracts on a monthly or yearly basis in and around Karnataka.

We are also suppliers of water entertainment, amusement, fountain and competition equipment. Our team is made up of well-experienced and qualified young professionals who strive to work hard for customer satisfaction.
